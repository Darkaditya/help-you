Questions

Do my customers need to install an app?

No. They open your link or scan the QR code in their phone browser.

Do customers have to log in to order?

No. They enter their name and mobile number. To see past orders they enter the same mobile number.

Which devices does it work on?

Phones, tablets and computers, in any modern browser.

Can another restaurant see my data?

No. Each restaurant's orders, menu and staff are kept in a separate space, and only your own logins can open it.
